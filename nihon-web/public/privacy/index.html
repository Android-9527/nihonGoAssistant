Privacy Policy (English)

NihonGo Assistant ("we", "our", or "the App") is a Japanese-learning application providing vocabulary, grammar, sentence study, pronunciation audio, tests, and a personal wordbook with spaced review. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: when you register, we collect your email address and an optional nickname. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrypt hash on our server — we never store your plaintext password.
  • Learning data: when you save words to your wordbook or take tests and review cards, we store the saved words, your correct/incorrect answers, mastery scores, and your review schedule. This data is linked to your account.
  • Session information: when you log in, we create a session token (valid for up to 30 days) so you stay signed in.
  • We do NOT collect: precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or biometric data. The App contains no third-party advertising SDK and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • provide the learning features you request (wordbook, tests, spaced review, progress statistics);
  • synchronize your learning progress across devices;
  • maintain the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except for the following service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud Platform — our servers and database are hosted on Google Cloud.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — used only to generate Japanese pronunciation audio for textbook content. Only textbook text is sent to this service; it receives no personal information or learning records.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored in a database on servers we control and transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes; session tokens expire after 30 days.
  • Access to personal data is limited to the minimum necessary to operate the service.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Account and learning data are retained while your account exists.
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated learning data at any time by emailing mtk20170119@gmail.com. We will delete the data within 30 days after verifying the request, except where retention is required by law.
  • Session tokens are automatically removed after expiration.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information (e.g., nickname, email);
  • Request deletion of your account and data;
  • Withdraw consent for processing by deleting your account.

To exercise these rights, contact us at mtk20170119@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The App uses browser/device local storage only to remember your language preference and your login session token. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. Clearing app data or logging out removes the stored token.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and material changes may also be announced in the App.

隐私政策（中文）

NihonGo Assistant（以下简称"我们"或"本应用"）是一款日语学习应用，提供单词、语法、 句子学习、发音音频、测试，以及带间隔复习的个人单词本。本隐私政策说明我们收集哪些信息、 如何使用这些信息，以及您拥有的选择权。

一、我们收集哪些信息

  • 账号信息：注册时我们收集您的邮箱地址和可选昵称。密码仅以单向 scrypt 哈希形式存储在我们的服务器上，我们不会存储明文密码。
  • 学习数据：当您收藏单词、进行测试或复习时，我们保存收藏的单词、答题对错、掌握度和复习计划。这些数据与您的账号关联。
  • 登录会话：登录后我们会生成一个会话令牌（最长 30 天有效），用于保持登录状态。
  • 我们不收集：精确位置、通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风录音、广告标识符或生物识别数据。本应用不含第三方广告 SDK，不跨应用或网站追踪您。

二、信息的使用目的

我们仅将信息用于：

  • 创建并保护您的账号；
  • 提供您请求的学习功能（单词本、测试、间隔复习、学习统计）；
  • 在设备间同步您的学习进度；
  • 维护服务的安全与稳定。

我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不会将其用于广告或用户画像。

三、信息共享与第三方服务

除以下代我们处理数据的服务商外，我们不会与第三方共享您的个人信息：

  • Google Cloud Platform — 我们的服务器与数据库托管在 Google Cloud。
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — 仅用于为教材内容生成日语发音音频。该服务只接收教材文本，不接收您的个人信息或学习记录。

如法律要求或为保护用户权利与安全，我们可能披露相关信息。

四、数据存储与安全

  • 数据存储在我们控制的服务器数据库中，并通过 HTTPS 传输。
  • 密码以 scrypt 哈希存储；会话令牌 30 天后自动过期。
  • 对个人数据的访问权限仅限于运营服务所必需的最小范围。

没有任何传输或存储方式是 100% 安全的，但我们会采取合理措施保护您的信息。

  • 您可以随时通过邮件 mtk20170119@gmail.com 申请删除账号及全部关联学习数据。我们将在核实请求后 30 天内删除，法律要求保留的除外。
